Agent payments are about to go off the record.

GhostTab lets AI agents pay for APIs over x402 with blind-signed credit. Fund once from Solana, Tempo, Ethereum or Zcash, then pay for every call without leaving a single on-chain trail back to your wallet.

✺

Fund once, from any chain

Top up your agent's tab with a single deposit on Solana, Tempo, Ethereum or a shielded Zcash note. That one transaction is the only thing the chain ever sees. Everything after it happens off the record.

✺

Spend without a trail

Your deposit mints blind-signed credit tokens. The mint signs them without ever seeing them, so no one, not even GhostTab, can link a paid API call back to the wallet that funded it.

✺

Your agent's strategy stays yours.

On plain x402, every paid call is a public receipt: which data feeds your trading agent buys, how often, and for how much. GhostTab turns that ledger into one deposit and one payout, so competitors have nothing to copy.

How a call gets paid

Your questions, answered.

What's x402?

x402 revives the HTTP 402 Payment Required status code so APIs can charge per request. The server answers with a price, the client retries with a payment header, and the data comes back. It's how AI agents pay for things without accounts or API keys.

What does GhostTab actually hide?

The link between who paid and what they paid for. Observers see one deposit into the mint and batched payouts to sellers. They can't see which wallet called which API, how often, or when.

Is the mint custodial?

In v1, yes. It works like a Cashu-style ecash mint, so keep balances small and working. Next on the roadmap is a federated mint that uses FROST threshold signatures, so no single operator can freeze or forge credit.

Which chains can I fund from?

Solana (USDC), Tempo stablecoins, Ethereum, and shielded Zcash. Credit is chain-agnostic once it's minted, so an agent funded on one chain can pay any GhostTab-enabled API.

How do sellers get paid?

Add one line of middleware, ghosttab.protect({ price: 0.01 }), to accept tokens. Sellers redeem them in batches and get paid in USDC on Solana.
